About the Project

The Waste to Wealth AI Motherboard Diagnostic Engine is a sustainable technology initiative designed to transform electronic waste into reusable value. Using a custom-trained YOLOv8 computer vision model, the platform automatically identifies hardware defects from motherboard images and evaluates refurbishment potential.

The system detects missing components, PCB scratches, corrosion, damaged PCIe slots, socket pin failures and assembly issues. Each defect is mapped to an economic valuation model that estimates restoration effort, repair feasibility and acquisition value.

This project helps recyclers, refurbishers, repair centers and e-waste management facilities make faster decisions while reducing manual inspection costs. By separating repairable hardware from scrap-only boards, the platform contributes to a circular economy and minimizes environmental impact.

Technologies: YOLOv8, Flask, OpenCV, Python, NumPy, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, Sustainable AI and E-Waste Analytics.
